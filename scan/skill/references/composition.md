# Follow a fact across patterns

Use the catalog as a set of connected questions. A feature can combine several mechanisms. A correct annotation on one destination does not settle the others.

## Establish the fact

Start with the feature's purpose and audience. Use the content map to separate value families and establish constraints at their producers. Identify the session actor, the subject of the displayed record, whether the content is intended for publication, and whether possession of a value grants access. Follow a producer when a rendering expression does not establish these facts. Distinguish a coordinator transcribing somebody else's information from a person entering their own information.

Do not infer private meaning solely from a variable name or infer public meaning solely from a constant. Fixed strings, ordering or selected classes can disclose a private predicate.

## Build a destination worklist

For each fact, follow this sequence until no new supported destination appears. A feature scope bounds the requested outcome, not access to dependencies: follow producers and renderers across the full available codebase. For application-wide work, reconcile closed feature worklists with a ledger of routes, entry points and capture integrations:

1. **Producer:** props, query, context, store, schema, user entry or server markup. Establish the subject and audience independently of the producer mechanism.
2. **Derivation:** shortened text, formatting, fallback, URL construction, aggregate, selected state or identity-bearing transformation. Carry the meaning through derivations; do not assume shortening or hashing makes it public.
3. **Destination:** native text, control state, attribute, style, media, document metadata, or a separately captured payload. Record separate channels on the same element.
4. **Owner:** actual DOM ancestor, slot, portal, shadow root, frame or recorder integration. Follow forwarding and merge precedence to the actual target.
5. **State:** first render, loading, resolved, empty, invalid, edited, revealed, stale, switched and unmounted. Select the applicable states from source, then inspect every relevant branch.
6. **Protection:** choose the smallest adequate treatment for that channel under the exact recorder contract. Preserve stronger protection and document any public content lost.

If a new copy, producer, branch or capture integration appears, add it to the worklist. Keep unavailable definitions and recorder capabilities explicit. A missing implementation does not erase a supported private meaning.

Use a compact working table when several destinations interact:

| Fact / subject | Producer evidence | Derivation / state | Destination and owner | Required treatment | Placement / runtime evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What the information means and whom it concerns | Literal producer or caller reference | Transformation or branch | Exact native or external channel | Based on the recorder contract | Verified, proposed, unresolved, or observed in the stated test |

## Compose cards deliberately

Read the catalog index once to identify relevant families. Follow each selected card's composition links when the source exhibits that mechanism. Do not load every card for every simple field. Before closing a feature, check for unexamined families: audience, derived facts, copies, detached owners, transitions, interactions, navigation metadata, external capture, control forwarding and evidence gaps.

For example, a display alias may be shortened in a shared cell, expanded in a detached tooltip, and echoed in the document title after a record switch. Review the native text, tooltip mount, title capture and transitional record separately. A wrapper around the cell supplies no evidence about the other destinations.

Keep fixed public controls as explicit counterexamples. A private destination and a public label can share an element. Classify their channels independently, then report the public loss if the smallest valid native block necessarily removes both. Preserve public callers of a shared component through an explicit, verified private context.

After the first trace, select focused review lenses from observed cues. Use their findings to expand or correct this worklist; do not create a second disconnected list of privacy claims. Challenge shared placements and public losses before closing the feature.

## Close with evidence

Use the existing decision record format. Put the relevant stable pattern IDs in the rationale; they explain the investigation and do not substitute for source evidence. Additional destinations absent from the inventory belong in `additional_findings`.

Separate these conclusions:

- The source establishes the information's meaning and subject.
- The source proposal reaches a specific native destination or requires a recorder-level control.
- The patch applies and compiles in the declared scope.
- The exercised recorder omits the private channel while retaining the selected public controls.

For the last claim, inspect emitted payloads from the first relevant event through changes, with baseline positives for the same channel and phase. An unresolved canvas, frame, metadata or plugin channel cannot receive protection credit from a DOM annotation alone. A successful synthetic source decision is evidence of reasoning on that case; it is not an executed recording result.
